Rules that shaped every card

Roster on the meeting date

Every attendance and compliance denominator calls wlt.roster_on(group_id, meeting_date). A woman who joined in month 6 must not make months 1 to 5 look worse. A card recomputing against today's roster is a review-blocking defect, the same class as computing a percentage inline.

Maturation, on phase age

The P2 gate needs 52 weeks since P1. In pilot month 6 no group can pass it. Every attainment denominator filters to groups old enough, and the rest are counted as not yet eligible. Skip this and the pilot publishes a collapse that has not happened.

Small numbers, restated for groups

Dire Dawa is allocated 15 groups. Suppression bands at 30 and 10 make it permanently provisional for the pre-pilot, and it is never ranked. Afar at 50 and Central Ethiopia at 45 are reportable but never league-tabled. State the consequence; do not drop the bands.

Money is a higher bar than counts

Ledger cards net reversals, because corrections are reversals and never edits. Fund adequacy stays in weeks of contribution, so it needs no re-indexing for inflation and stays comparable across regions. Assert both.

Thresholds come from policy

Every threshold renders from wlt.policy_parameter, never typed into a card. Otherwise a card and the gate that blocks the group will eventually disagree, and the facilitator will believe the card.

Protection stays off group cards

The person-only constraint stops a GBV disclosure landing on a group record. It does not stop a linkage card aggregating protection referrals into something a facilitator can drill into. Every linkage card filters the type out in SQL.
