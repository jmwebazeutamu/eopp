How a group moves

Four phases. Every gate computed from the ledger, then approved by the woreda

A facilitator cannot advance a group by ticking a box. The conditions below are calculated from recorded meetings, savings and loans. When they are all met the group becomes eligible, and the woreda office approves or refuses it.

Forming

Roster and committee in place, bylaws recorded, first savings meeting closed with a balanced till.

Roster 15–25Chair, secretary, treasurer

Phase 1: saving

Weekly saving establishes the habit and the fund. Attendance and compliance are measured, not asserted.

Adherence ≥90%Attendance ≥80%10+ meetings

Phase 2: lending

Internal lending starts. One bad loan holds the whole group. PAR30 must be zero at the gate.

Fund ≥12 weeks1 loan cycle closedPAR30 = 0

Phase 3: CLA

Eight or more eligible groups in one kebele each elect two delegates. The event stays open until every pair is recorded.

8+ eligible SHGs2 delegates eachWoreda approval

Phase 4: federation

Ten or more CLAs, each running a year or more. Only here does an external credit facility become possible.

10+ CLAs12+ months each

Who signs in

Five roles, and a wall between recording and approving

Permissions are scoped by geography and by the groups you are assigned. You land on the screen your job starts from.

WLT facilitator

Assigned groups

Forms groups, runs meeting capture, keeps the savings and loan ledger. Records, never approves.

Lands on: group roster and readiness

Woreda FSCO

Own woreda

Approves phase advancement, CLA formation and service linkages. Cannot approve a group they recorded.

Lands on: approval queue, oldest first

CF or DA

Own kebele

Existing PSNP field staff. Verifies enrolment exceptions and supports groups in the kebele.

Lands on: kebele group list

Built for the conditions

Designed around weekly meetings under a tree

  • Offline first, not offline tolerant

    A meeting is recorded where it happens. Attendance, savings and repayments are written locally and synced later. Group selection requires at least one member with a device.

  • Records stay in country

    Ledgers, bylaws and photographs sit in self-hosted storage under government control, not a third-party cloud.

  • Built for low literacy

    Literacy level is a recorded selection criterion, so the interface and the printed group records are designed against it rather than around it.

One platform, two programmes

WLT ships first. Youth employment referrals follow

The registry, the geography hierarchy, the permissions model and the referral engine are shared. Youth employment case management is built on the same foundations in a later phase, so a young woman in a Self Help Group and a young person in a training placement are the same person record, never two.

The separation is deliberate in the other direction too. A facilitator with access to a group roster does not thereby gain access to those women's case files.
